# Privacy Notice — Kargo Hiring

This notice explains what happens to your information if you apply for a role at Kargo and we use
this tool to review your application. It's written in plain language, not legal boilerplate.

## What we collect

When you apply, we collect whatever is in the CV/resume you send us — typically your name, email,
phone number, and your work history, education, and skills.

## How we use it

- Your name, email, and phone number are stored in one place, separate from everything else, and are
  never sent to any AI model.
- The rest of your CV (work history, skills, etc.) has your name, email, and phone number stripped out
  of it automatically before it's sent to Google's Gemini AI, which scores it against our hiring rubric
  and helps draft a reply. The AI never sees who you are — only what you've done.
- A human (the founder) reviews every AI-generated score and email draft before anything is sent to
  you. The AI recommends; a person decides.
- We do not sell, share, or use your data for anything other than evaluating your application.

## How long we keep it

We don't have a fixed auto-deletion schedule today — your application stays on file so we can consider
you for this and future roles, unless you ask us to remove it (see below).

We do **not** keep a copy of the original file you sent us (the PDF/DOCX itself). Once we've extracted
the text we need, the original file is discarded — only the extracted, redacted text is kept.

## Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

- **See what we have on file for you**, or
- **Have your data permanently deleted** — this removes your contact details, scores, interview notes,
  and any draft or sent emails, with no copy kept anywhere.

To do either, email **siddhant_bassi@pg27.mesaschool.co**. We'll action it directly — there's no
automated self-service flow for this today, since applicants don't have accounts or logins on this
system; a person on our side handles every request by hand.

## Who sees your information

Only the founder has access to this system. There is no recruiting team, no third party, and no other
account with access to applicant data.

## Questions

If anything here is unclear, or you want to know more about how a specific decision was made, email
**siddhant_bassi@pg27.mesaschool.co**.
