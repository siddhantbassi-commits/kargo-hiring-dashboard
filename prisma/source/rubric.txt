KARGO HIRING RUBRIC

PRODUCT MANAGER

Independent Ownership: What a strong candidate looks like: Owns
meaningful work from problem definition through implementation and
follow-through, with evidence that they personally made key decisions
rather than relying on senior direction. Weight: 25%

Problem Initiative: What a strong candidate looks like: Provides at
least one concrete example of independently noticing a problem,
inefficiency or unmet need and initiating a solution rather than simply
executing assigned work. Weight: 20%

Measurable Impact: What a strong candidate looks like: Connects their
actions to observable outcomes such as adoption, revenue, retention,
reduced support volume, faster processing or another quantified result.
Weight: 20%

Organisational Leverage: What a strong candidate looks like: Has created
a process, tool, framework, document or working practice that was
adopted and used by others beyond themselves. Weight: 15%

Evidence & Learning: What a strong candidate looks like: Demonstrates
testing assumptions, observing results and changing direction when
evidence shows the original approach is not working; explicit examples
of stopping or redirecting work are particularly strong evidence.
Weight: 20%

Total: 100%

SENIOR PRODUCT MANAGER

Independent Ownership: What a strong candidate looks like: Shows
repeated evidence of independently owning consequential work end-to-end
and making important decisions without needing senior direction or
approval, including across multiple stakeholders and ambiguous
situations. Weight: 30%

Problem Initiative: What a strong candidate looks like: Repeatedly
identifies important problems or opportunities independently and
initiates action, with evidence that they helped determine what should
be solved rather than only how to execute it. Weight: 15%

Measurable Impact: What a strong candidate looks like: Shows multiple
examples where personally owned decisions led to measurable customer,
business or operational outcomes, with a clear connection between their
action and the result. Weight: 20%

Organisational Leverage: What a strong candidate looks like: Has built
systems, processes, frameworks or ways of working that other people or
teams adopted and continued using, improving how the wider organisation
operates. Weight: 20%

Evidence & Learning: What a strong candidate looks like: Demonstrates
making consequential decisions with incomplete information, examining
outcomes honestly and changing course when evidence contradicts the
original assumption, including learning from failed or underperforming
work. Weight: 15%

Total: 100%
